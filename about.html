Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue was founded on the belief that every animal deserves a safe, healthy, and loving home. Our organization is dedicated to helping animals in need by connecting them with caring families and providing support through adoption, foster care, and community involvement.

We believe that rescuing animals is a community effort. By working with volunteers, foster families, and animal lovers, we strive to improve the lives of animals and help them find their forever homes.

Our Core Values

  • Compassion:We treat every animal with kindness, patience, and respect.
  • Responsible Care:We promote the health, safety, and well-being of every animal in our care.
  • Community:We believe volunteers, foster families, and adopters play an important role in animal rescue.
  • Commitment: We work toward finding safe, loving, and permanent homes for animals in need.
  • Accessibility:We strive to create welcoming opportunities for people from different backgrounds and abilities to support our mission.

Our Partners and Supporters

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is grateful for the support of volunteers, foster families, adopters, local community members, and animal welfare advocates. Their time, compassion, and contributions help us care for animals and connect them with loving homes.

We also recognize the importance of collaboration with veterinary professionals, local shelters, and community organizations that share our commitment to animal welfare. Together, we can make a positive difference in the lives of animals.

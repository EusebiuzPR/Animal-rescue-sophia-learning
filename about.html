Our Services

Twin Cities Animal Rescue provides shelter, medical care, and adoption services for animals in need.
Our goal is to help every animal find a safe and loving home.

Adoption Process Overview

We help rescued animals find loving, permanent homes. Our adoption process helps families choose a pet that fits their lifestyle and needs.
We guide adopters through each step to ensure every animal receives the care, safety, and love it deserves.

Foster Program Overview

Our foster program provides temporary homes for rescued animals while they wait to be adopted. Foster families offer a safe environment, attention, and care to help animals feel comfortable and prepare for their forever homes.
Fostering also creates space for us to rescue more animals in need.

Volunteer Role Descriptions

Volunteers play an important role in supporting our animal rescue mission. They can help care for animals, organize supplies, assist with adoption events, and support daily shelter activities.
Every volunteer contribution helps us provide better care and connect more animals with loving families.

Our Mission:

Our mission is to rescue, protect, and care for animals in need by providing shelter, medical attention, and loving homes. We are committed to promoting animal welfare and helping every animal find a safe and happy future.

Our Impact

  • 500+ animals rescued from unsafe or neglected environments.
  • 350+ animals adopted into loving homes.
  • 200+ animals provided with medical care and rehabilitation.
  • 100+ volunteers helping us improve the lives of animals.

How You Can Help

You can make a difference in the lives of animals in need! Here are some ways to support our mission:

  • Adopt: Give a rescued animal a loving, permanent home.
  • Donate: Contribute money, food, blankets, or supplies.
  • Volunteer: Help care for animals and support rescue efforts.
  • Spread Awareness: Share our mission with friends and family to encourage responsible pet ownership.

Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue was founded on the belief that every animal deserves a safe, healthy, and loving home. Our organization is dedicated to helping animals in need by connecting them with caring families and providing support through adoption, foster care, and community involvement.

We believe that rescuing animals is a community effort. By working with volunteers, foster families, and animal lovers, we strive to improve the lives of animals and help them find their forever homes.

Our Core Values

  • Compassion:We treat every animal with kindness, patience, and respect.
  • Responsible Care:We promote the health, safety, and well-being of every animal in our care.
  • Community:We believe volunteers, foster families, and adopters play an important role in animal rescue.
  • Commitment: We work toward finding safe, loving, and permanent homes for animals in need.
  • Accessibility:We strive to create welcoming opportunities for people from different backgrounds and abilities to support our mission.

Our Partners and Supporters

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is grateful for the support of volunteers, foster families, adopters, local community members, and animal welfare advocates. Their time, compassion, and contributions help us care for animals and connect them with loving homes.

We also recognize the importance of collaboration with veterinary professionals, local shelters, and community organizations that share our commitment to animal welfare. Together, we can make a positive difference in the lives of animals.

Our Services

Twin Cities Animal Rescue provides shelter, medical care, and adoption services for animals in need.
Our goal is to help every animal find a safe and loving home.

Adoption Process Overview

We help rescued animals find loving, permanent homes. Our adoption process helps families choose a pet that fits their lifestyle and needs.
We guide adopters through each step to ensure every animal receives the care, safety, and love it deserves.

Foster Program Overview

Our foster program provides temporary homes for rescued animals while they wait to be adopted. Foster families offer a safe environment, attention, and care to help animals feel comfortable and prepare for their forever homes.
Fostering also creates space for us to rescue more animals in need.

Volunteer Role Descriptions

Volunteers play an important role in supporting our animal rescue mission. They can help care for animals, organize supplies, assist with adoption events, and support daily shelter activities.
Every volunteer contribution helps us provide better care and connect more animals with loving families.
